Branded Email Signatures for Gmail & Email Clients

How to add to Gmail in 30 seconds:

  1. Click the brown "Copy Signature" button next to your favorite design below.
  2. Open Gmail and click the Gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll down on the General tab to Signature and click Create new.
  4. Click into the text box and press Cmd + V (Mac) or Ctrl + V (Windows).
  5. Under Signature defaults, select your signature for "For new emails use" and "On reply/forward use".
  6. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
